Welcome to your San Diego home base! This brand new 3 bedroom, 3.5 bath townhome sits in Golden Hill, just minutes from Balboa Park, the San Diego Zoo, and downtown.

You'll find a primary suite with a king bed and electric fireplace, two queen bedrooms, an 85" Samsung Frame TV, a fully equipped kitchen, a coffee bar, central A/C, fast WiFi, and an in-unit washer and dryer. Park in the private 2-car garage with EV charging, and help yourself to the complimentary baby gear if you're traveling with little ones.

Please treat the home as your own, and message me through the Airbnb app anytime you need anything. Enjoy your stay!

Tips

For Your Stay

Step outside

The 32nd St Canyon trailhead is right across the street, and the cafes and restaurants of Golden Hill and South Park are just a few minutes away.

Skip the parking hassle

Downtown, Petco Park, and Balboa Park are all under 10 minutes away. On game nights and weekends, a rideshare is usually easier than parking downtown.

Traveling with kids?

We have a crib, a Pack 'n Play, a booster high chair, and baby gates ready for you.

Places

To See

Balboa Park & Zoo

Gardens, museums, Spanish architecture, and the world famous San Diego Zoo. About 7 minutes away.

Gaslamp & Petco Park

Catch a Padres game or explore downtown's best dining and nightlife, about 7 minutes away.

Cabrillo Monument

Sweeping views of the bay, the city, and the Pacific from Point Loma. Go for sunset.

La Jolla Cove

Sea lions, tide pools, and ocean views. About 20 minutes north and worth the drive.

Things

To Do

Hiking

Explore the 32nd St Canyon trail right in the neighborhood, or the Florida Canyon trails in Balboa Park.

Beaches

Coronado, Mission Beach, and Pacific Beach are all within 15 to 20 minutes. Pack the beach towels and go.

Surfing

Rent a board or book a lesson at Pacific Beach or Mission Beach. Great for beginners.

Snorkeling

La Jolla Cove has clear water, sea lions, and garibaldi fish. Kayak and snorkel tours run year round.

Golf

Balboa Park Golf Course is about 5 minutes away with city views. Torrey Pines is a must for serious golfers.

Activities for

Kids

San Diego Zoo

Pandas, polar bears, and the Skyfari aerial tram. About 7 minutes away in Balboa Park. Plan for a full day.

Balboa Park rides

Ride the historic carousel and the miniature railroad next to the zoo. Quick, cheap, and little kids love it.

Fleet Science Center

Hands-on science exhibits and a dome theater in Balboa Park. A great pick for a hot or rainy afternoon.

USS Midway Museum

Climb into real cockpits on a giant aircraft carrier on the downtown waterfront, about 10 minutes away.

Getting

Around

By foot

Walk to the shops, coffee, and restaurants on 25th St and 30th St. Balboa Park is about a 20 minute walk.

Rideshare

Uber and Lyft are the easiest option. Expect about 15 minutes to the airport and under 10 minutes to Petco Park or the Gaslamp.

Bikes & scooters

Lime and other e-bikes and scooters are parked all over Golden Hill. Unlock them in the app and ride to Balboa Park or downtown in minutes.

Trolley

The MTS Trolley Orange Line runs along Commercial St just south of the house and connects to downtown, the Gaslamp, and Petco Park.

Bus

MTS buses run along Broadway, 25th St, and 30th St. Pay with the PRONTO app or a PRONTO card.
